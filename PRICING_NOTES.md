# Fare and capacity model

Updated 8 September 2026. Website figures are pre-booking estimates, include 9% Dutch VAT, and become final when Anjum confirms the request.

## What the market currently charges

- The Dutch 2026 maximum street-taxi tariff is €4.31 start + €3.17/km + €0.52/minute for a car carrying up to four passengers. Pre-booked journeys may use an agreed fixed price.
- Faircab advertises Schiphol fixed fares of Amsterdam €55, Utrecht €80, Den Haag €90 and Rotterdam €95. Standard luggage is included up to two large suitcases plus hand luggage.
- SchipholRide advertises Comfort fares of Amsterdam €50–€55, Utrecht €80, Den Haag €80 and Rotterdam €125. These include VAT, luggage and Schiphol costs.
- The useful competitive range for Schiphol–Rotterdam is therefore about €95–€125. Anjum's normal route-based result is designed to sit inside that range.

## Rates implemented

City and regional rides:

- €4.50 base
- First 10 km: €2.05/km
- Next 20 km: €1.55/km
- Remaining distance: €1.15/km
- €0.34/minute, with a 10% duration buffer for ordinary traffic variation
- €20 minimum

Schiphol transfers:

- €18 base
- First 20 km: €1.15/km
- Next 30 km: €0.85/km
- Remaining distance: €0.60/km
- €0.18/minute
- Route under 12 km: minimum €39 to Schiphol or €49 from Schiphol
- Route from 12 km to under 17 km: minimum €45 to Schiphol or €52.50 from Schiphol
- Route from 17 km through 25 km: minimum €50 to Schiphol or €55 from Schiphol
- Regional airport minimums before positioning: Rotterdam €85, Den Haag €80, Utrecht €75, Almere €75 and Haarlem €40

Allowances:

- Booking 6–24 hours before pickup: €5 per affected leg
- Booking under 6 hours before pickup: €10 per affected leg
- Non-airport pickup from 23:00 through 05:59: €5 per leg
- No separate airport night or weekday peak surcharge
- When either endpoint is over 15 km from central Amsterdam, €0.75 is added for each excess straight-line kilometre. Using the farther endpoint covers likely unpaid positioning or the empty return to the Amsterdam home market.
- Return bookings equal the two separately calculated legs; no automatic return discount is applied.
- The final amount is rounded up to the nearest €0.50.

## Rotterdam example

An OSRM route check from Schiphol to Rotterdam Centraal is about 59.4 km and 49 minutes. The airport formula is roughly €81, so the explicit Rotterdam minimum first raises it to €85. Rotterdam Centraal is about 57.5 km straight-line from central Amsterdam, producing a €32 regional-positioning allowance. The normal estimate is therefore about **€117 including 9% VAT** before any short-notice addition.

The €85 requested threshold remains a hard minimum. It should not be the normal selling price for a solo Amsterdam-based driver because €85 contains only €77.98 excluding VAT. If the driver returns empty, the vehicle can cover about 119 km. At the conservative €0.50 actual-kilometre reserve below, that consumes about €59.50 and leaves only €18.48 for roughly two hours of driving, loading, waiting and administration. At €117, the pre-income-tax contribution is about €47.84 on the same assumptions, or roughly €24/hour.

## Vehicle capacity shown to customers

The SEAT Leon ST specification lists 587 litres of boot space with the passenger seats in use. The 2015 model literature lists up to 1,470 litres with the rear seats folded, but folded seats reduce the passenger capacity and should not be advertised as the normal four-passenger luggage space.

The website therefore makes this conservative promise:

- Up to four passengers
- 587 L boot
- Maximum standard luggage with all passenger seats available: two large suitcases plus two cabin bags
- More, unusually shaped or oversized luggage must be confirmed before pickup

Litres cannot be converted into an exact suitcase count because luggage shapes and dimensions vary. The booking request now records a luggage category so Anjum can reject or clarify loads that will not fit safely.

## Vehicle-cost assumptions

The 2015 SEAT Leon ST 1.6 TDI is efficient, but published combined figures do not reflect taxi idling, Amsterdam traffic or an older vehicle. Use 5.5 L/100 km as the planning consumption.

At the CBS national diesel average of €2.326/litre on 31 August 2026, that assumption costs about €0.128 per driven kilometre in fuel. Reserving another €0.35–€0.40/km for tyres, servicing, repairs, depreciation, taxi insurance, motor tax, cleaning and compliance produces a prudent total vehicle reserve of about €0.48–€0.53 per actual kilometre. The pricing review uses €0.50/km as its centre point.

These rates should be checked against actual costs after 20–30 completed rides. Record paid distance, empty distance, total working time, parking, fuel and the final fare. Increase the regional positioning rate if empty running is consistently high; reduce it only when repeat return work provides reliable paid backhauls.
